#### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: 
TokoAI suffers from a severe identity split across routes. The marketing landing page (`/`) is rich with Cambodian commerce specificity—featuring vibrant TikTok pink (`#FF3B6B`), cyan (`#14F1D9`), authentic Khmer dialogue previews, and Bakong KHQR cards. However, the moment a seller enters `/login`, `/register`, or `/dashboard`, all cultural and brand character vanishes. The dashboard is styled as a generic, harsh, pitch-black developer terminal with hardcoded `#0A0A0A`, `#111`, and `#222` palettes, completely divorced from the lively TikTok retail persona.

**Deterministic scan**:
- Automated scan flagged `overused-font` on `globals.css` (line 82) for generic Inter font usage without distinct personality.
- Architectural collision: Two conflicting component sets exist simultaneously (`components/ui/design-system.tsx` with hardcoded dark styles vs. `components/ui/button.tsx`, `card.tsx` with Base UI and Tailwind tokens).
- Dead code: Pre-styled responsive components (`Sidebar.tsx`, `Topbar.tsx`, `MobileBottomNav.tsx`) are completely orphaned while `dashboard/layout.tsx` reimplements an inflexible inline sidebar.
- Global CSS collision: `globals.css` forces `* { border-color: #333; }` and `body { background-color: #000; }`, overriding the HSL light tokens configured in `:root`.

#### Overall Impression
The core business concept—autonomous spoken Khmer AI commerce with real-time Bakong KHQR checkout—is compelling and clearly differentiated. However, the frontend currently behaves like two different applications hastily stitched together: an attractive TikTok commerce landing page on top of a generic, dark-mode developer console. Unifying the design system and bringing authentic TikTok vibrancy and mobile-first affordances to the dashboard will immediately elevate this into a world-class SaaS product.

#### What's Working
1. **Compelling Landing Page Narrative**: Clear value proposition addressing midnight order drop-offs, Khmeringlish parsing, and instant Bakong KHQR generation.
2. **Interactive Elements on Marketing Page**: The ROI calculator and the realistic spoken Khmer chat mockup immediately prove the product's value.
3. **Core Workflow Architecture**: The AI Takeover / Pause toggle in the inbox gives merchants essential psychological control over autonomous sales bots.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P0] Design System Split & Conflicting Global Styles**:
  - **Why it matters**: Breaks visual trust. The landing page uses vibrant TikTok pink, cyan, and clean mist backgrounds, while the dashboard is pitch-black `#000` with hard `#333` borders. Global CSS overrides break theme variables.
  - **Fix**: Consolidate `components/ui/design-system.tsx` into standard Tailwind design tokens. Transition the dashboard to a cohesive, warm dark palette with TikTok pink/cyan accents, and eliminate hardcoded `#000` / `#333` body overrides.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

- **[P1] Orphaned Mobile Navigation & Broken Responsive Layout**:
  - **Why it matters**: Over 85% of Cambodian retail sellers manage their shops on mobile devices. `Sidebar.tsx`, `Topbar.tsx`, and `MobileBottomNav.tsx` are completely orphaned; `dashboard/layout.tsx` uses an inline desktop-only sidebar that is unwieldy on phones.
  - **Fix**: Wire up `MobileBottomNav.tsx` and responsive layouts into `dashboard/layout.tsx` with touch-friendly 44px+ hit targets.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt`

- **[P2] Amateurish Error Handling & Raw Database IDs**:
  - **Why it matters**: Forms still trigger native browser `alert('Settings saved!')` popups. Orders and inbox tables expose raw foreign keys (`User 12`, `Customer c9d24a`) instead of customer names, TikTok handles, or phone numbers.
  - **Fix**: Replace native alerts with Sonner toast notifications. Format customer entities with avatars, phone numbers, and location badges.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

- **[P3] Unoptimized Khmer Typography & Missing Dual Currency Display**:
  - **Why it matters**: Kantumruy Pro is loaded but lacks adjusted line-height (`leading-relaxed`), causing Khmer diacritics to clip. In addition, dashboard metrics show USD only, ignoring the essential Cambodian dual-currency standard ($USD / ៛KHR).
  - **Fix**: Apply dedicated typography classes for Khmer text with comfortable line-height, and render dual currency ($USD & ៛KHR) across orders, analytics, and chats.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable typeset`

#### Persona Red Flags
- **Alex (Power User / High-Volume Seller)**: Cannot batch-dispatch orders (must click J&T/VET buttons one by one). No keyboard shortcuts in the Unified Inbox to switch chats or trigger AI takeover.
- **Jordan (First-Timer Boutique Owner)**: Intimidated by developer jargon ("Tokens Burned", "Inference Active") and pitch-black terminal styling. Confused by abrupt browser `alert()` dialogs.
- **Casey (Mobile Merchant at the Market)**: Opens dashboard on smartphone; lacks a bottom navigation bar, tables overflow horizontally, and critical actions require excessive zooming and panning.

#### Minor Observations
- Missing empty state illustrations in Inbox, Orders, and Knowledge Base when zero records exist.
- Form validation on the Register page uses a complex regex title tooltip instead of clear, inline helper checklist bullets.
- Chat message bubbles in the inbox have stark contrast with no subtle bubble gradients or rounded avatar badges.
